Girl, Boy, Bakla, Tomboy
Setting
The film is set in modern-day Philippines, mainly in homes, neighborhoods, and places where the siblings grew up separately. The setting reflects Filipino family culture and everyday life.

Characters
Main characters are the quadruplets played by Vice Ganda:
Girl – feminine and emotional
Boy – tough and masculine
Bakla – funny and openly gay
Tomboy – strong and masculine-presenting girl
Other characters include their parents and people connected to their lives.

Plot
The story follows quadruplets who were separated because their father believed a superstition that they would bring bad luck if raised together. As they grow older with different personalities and identities, they eventually reunite and learn to accept and support each other as a family.
